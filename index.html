About us

Herbs were always part of our home.

Back in the late 1990s, my nani kept a shelf of dried herbs and oils in her kitchen. Every small ache and every dull skin day had a home remedy, and she explained why it worked. That habit of asking "what is in this and why?" stayed with me.

OVNTA WELLNESSS began from that idea: bring traditional Indian herbal knowledge together with modern, easy-to-use wellness and skincare. The name on our emblem says it plainly, "Nature se Future tak".

Come in, call or message us. We would rather explain a product properly than sell you one you do not need.

  • Herbal-first approach to wellness and skincare
  • Simple three-step skincare routine, easy to follow
  • Products for men, women and the whole family
  • Honest guidance before you buy anything
  • A genuine business opportunity, explained openly
